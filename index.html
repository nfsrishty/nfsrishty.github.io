Research

01

Evaluating Automatic Metrics and LLM-as-Judge for Clinical Text Summarization

Undergraduate thesis
  • Evaluated ROUGE, BLEU, BERTScore, and LLM-as-a-Judge for clinical text summarization using 300 MIMIC-IV cases and 900 generated summaries.
  • Used Qwen2.5-7B/14B, BART-Large-CNN, prompting, correlation analysis, case-clustered bootstrap, and sensitivity analysis for metric evaluation.
  • Found BERTScore to show the strongest and most stable agreement with LLM-based judgments, while the LLM judge aligned more strongly with human ratings than the tested automatic metrics.
